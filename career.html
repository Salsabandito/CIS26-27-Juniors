User Experience (UX) Designers

The main job of a UX designer is to make a webpage easy and enjoyable to use, as well as accessible and intuitive.

What They do

They work with the User Interface (UI) Designer, and of course the rest of the developing team, on the design of the website, ensuring it is convinent for users to use.

Skills You Need

Technical Skills

  • Prototyping
  • Wireframing
  • Research
  • Information architecture

People Skills

  • Communication
  • Empathy
  • Collaboration
  • Critical thinking

How to Get Started

  1. You can take part in Googles "Google UX Design Professional Certificate Program" to learn skills and earn a:
  2. Google UX Design Professional Certificate
  3. Most job listings for entry level UX Designers do not require advanced degrees, however, having a Master's or even just a Bachler's degree in related fields will look good on resumes.
  4. The entry level position title you can expect for this industry is a Junior UX Designer.

Pay and Job Outlook

As online technology grows, the jobs that uphold the platforms grow as well. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, UX desinger jobs are projected to grow by 5% in the coming years.

Where You Could Work

In our modern age, almost everything is online and has some form of an online webpage. You could pick up a job at almost any company. However, some specific listings from the site Linkedin include an entry level position at "FIDx," and a Mid-Senior level postion at "Siemens Digital Industries Software."
